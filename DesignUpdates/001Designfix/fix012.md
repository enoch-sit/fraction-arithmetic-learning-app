# Fix 012: Non-Functional Buttons「上一步」and「重看」

## Problem
In the apps 「異分母分數加法」,「分數乘法教學」,「異分母分數除法」, the buttons 「上一步」(previous step) and「重看」(review) are not functional. Consider deleting them, together with「異分母分數減法」.

## Affected Apps
- Addition (異分母分數加法)
- Subtraction (異分母分數減法) - mentioned for consistency
- Multiplication (分數乘法教學)
- Division (異分母分數除法)

## Current State
- Buttons「上一步」and「重看」exist in UI
- Clicking them does nothing
- Creates user frustration and confusion

## Decision Required
Choose one approach:
1. **Remove the buttons** (recommended if truly non-functional)
2. **Implement the functionality** (if they serve a purpose)

## Recommended Solution
**Remove non-functional buttons** to avoid user confusion.
